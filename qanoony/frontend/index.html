What are the requirements for a fixed-term employment contract in Egypt?

10:42 AM done_all
smart_toy
Art. 104

According to the Egyptian Labor Law, a fixed-term contract must be in writing and specified for a certain duration. If the duration is not specified, the contract may be deemed indefinite under certain circumstances.

Key requirements include:

  • Clear identification of both parties (Employer and Employee).
  • A defined start and end date for the legal relationship.
  • A detailed description of the job role and responsibilities.
  • Explicit mention of the agreed salary and benefits.

Failure to renew a fixed-term contract in writing while continuing the relationship may convert it into an open-ended contract.

"Article 104: The fixed-term employment contract shall terminate by the expiry of its term. If its term is continued after its expiry by its parties, it shall be considered as an indefinite contract renewal."
